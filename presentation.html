The Autonomous Commerce Shift & The Latin American Advantage

Autonomous AI agents are shifting from passive text generation to executing direct commercial transactions. By integrating CodeSpar on top of Latin America's instant financial rails (Pix), we unlock frictionless conversational commerce and automated supply-chain procurement with zero infrastructure risk.

Mandates & HITL

Autonomous spend threshold + step-up human approval queue for supplier settlement.

🛡️ Agent Mandates & Spend Authority

Agents never receive raw bank credentials. Instead, all outbound payouts must satisfy a cryptographically verifiable Agent Mandate policy:

  • • Per-Transaction Limit: e.g. Max R$ 200.00 for autonomous clearance.
  • • Merchant Whitelist: Only registered suppliers matching verified CNPJs.
  • • Approval Queue Escalation: Any order > threshold is routed for Human-in-the-Loop review.
